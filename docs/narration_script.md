# Intro Narration Script
Target length: about 65-75 seconds. Tone: low, tired, calm, like someone remembering the worst day of their life.

## Part 1: Before (0:00 - 0:15)
"Willow Creek was a quiet town. Mountain air, church bells, neighbors who waved from their porches. Nobody locked their doors."

[pause 2 seconds]

## Part 2: The Outbreak (0:15 - 0:40)
"Then the sickness came. First it was just a few people, feverish and confused. Within a day, the hospital was overrun. By nightfall, the sirens stopped, because there was no one left to answer them."

[pause 2 seconds]

## Part 3: The Fall (0:40 - 0:55)
"The ones who changed... they didn't stop. They didn't sleep. They hunted anything that still breathed. We ran with whatever we could carry."

[pause 2 seconds]

## Part 4: The Escape (0:55 - 1:12)
"There's one road out, a path winding up through the mountains to the evacuation point. Everyone who stays behind is already lost. I'm not staying. I'm getting out of here."

[pause 3 seconds, then silence]
